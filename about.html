ABOUT

IPO information, made simpler.

IPO Radar is an independent information project designed to help people discover Indian IPOs and understand common IPO numbers such as price bands, lot sizes, subscription, GMP and key dates.

Our approach

We prefer clear labels, visible caveats and links to primary documents over hype or guaranteed-return language.

Primary verification

For official issue documents, investors should check regulator and exchange sources such as SEBI and NSE. NSE publishes public offer documents and issue information, while SEBI maintains public issue filings.

Not investment advice

IPO Radar does not provide personalised investment advice or guarantee listing gains, allotment or returns.
