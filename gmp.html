IPO GMP Tracker

Grey Market Premium is an unofficial market indicator. It is not an exchange price, not guaranteed, and can change quickly.

How to read GMP

If an IPO price is ₹100 and the unofficial GMP is ₹20, some market participants may quote an indicative ₹120 level. This is only a calculation example—not a prediction.

Never treat GMP as a substitute for reading the offer document, valuation, risks or subscription data.
